Time Until — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Time Until is a countdown and count-up app with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

Time Until does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • The events you create (titles, dates, emoji, colors, reminder settings and, for habit trackers, the amount and price you enter).
  • Photos you choose as backgrounds. They are copied into the app's private storage on your device so widgets can show them.
  • Local notifications you turn on. They are scheduled by your device and are not sent through any server.

The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Purchases

The optional Premium unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple may share anonymous purchase information with us as part of App Store reporting.

Photos and notifications permissions

Photos are only read when you pick one in the app. Notification permission is requested only when you save an event with reminders on. You can change both in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
